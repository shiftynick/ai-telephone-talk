ACT TWO · THE GAME

Rules of telephone,
except everyone is an LLM.

  • A real photo from your phone or a starting sentence
  • Step 1: an AI describes it · Step 2: another AI draws it back
  • Repeat… each step sees only its predecessor — pure telephone
  • Finish with a video animation (or not)

The audience watches meaning drift — or survive, which is honestly scarier.

HOW IT RUNS

Local-first
Fastify + SQLite + Vite on your laptop. The phone joins over your Wi-Fi via a QR code; nothing is tunneled to the internet.
But not offline
Every step goes to OpenRouter (text + vision + images) or fal.ai (video). Keys stay in a gitignored .env.
The invariant
Each step gets its immediate predecessor + one instruction. No history, no original photo. Provable from the actual provider payloads.
Hall of drift
Presets, adventure mode, autoplay, keyframe video… and it records every run so tonight’s drift lives on.
